# How to edit your website

## Open the editor
Go to **yourwebsite.com/admin** (or click **Admin** at the very bottom of the website) and sign in with your password.
The editor opens on the right. The website stays visible on the left and changes as you type.

## Change something
1. Pick a tab along the top of the editor: **Brand & contact**, **Opening slideshow**, **Services**, **Work** and so on.
   The website scrolls to that section.
2. Type your changes. Lists (slides, services, projects, questions) open when you click their name.
   Use **↑ ↓** to reorder, **⧉** to copy, and **×** twice to delete.
3. Press **Publish**. Your visitors now see the new version.

Not happy? Before publishing, use **⋯ → Undo unsaved changes**.

## Photos
- Click **Upload** under any photo and choose a file from your phone or computer. Large photos are shrunk automatically.
- **Library** reuses a photo you uploaded before.
- On slideshow photos, **click the preview** to choose where the slide opens from (put the dot on the couple, the stage or the product).

## Add a new project to your portfolio
**Work → Add project.** Add a photo, title, category and description.
For a video, set **Type** to *Film / video*, paste the **YouTube link**, and add the length (for example 3:48).
Tick **Show in selected work** to put it in the big side-scrolling gallery.

## Bookings
Open the **Enquiries** tab to see every booking sent from your website. Each one is also emailed to you.
Tap **WhatsApp** to reply straight away.

## Hide a section
**Before & after**, **Graphic design** and **Content & marketing** each have a **Show this section** switch.

## Safety net
Every time you publish, the previous version is saved automatically.
**⋯ → Download a backup** saves a copy to your computer; **Restore from a backup** brings it back.

## Forgot your password?
Ask your web developer, or delete the file `data/auth.json` in your hosting file manager and open /admin again.
